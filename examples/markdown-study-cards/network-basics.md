# Transport

<!-- qard-id: network-basics-tcp -->
> [!qard]- What does TCP provide?
> TCP provides **reliable, ordered delivery** of data.
>
> - Lost data can be retransmitted.
> - Received data is put back in order.

<!-- qard-id: network-basics-udp -->
> [!qard]- Does UDP guarantee delivery?
> No. UDP does not guarantee delivery or ordering.
